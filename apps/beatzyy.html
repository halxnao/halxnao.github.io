Beatzyy

Slowed Reverb Player

Beatzyy is an offline music player for Android that lets you adjust playback speed, apply reverb effects, customize audio and personalize the music playback interface.

  • Android
  • Paid
  • Google Play
Get it on Google Play

Features

  • Slowed playback from 0.65× to 1.0×.
  • Nightcore playback from 1.15× to 1.4×.
  • Reverb effects with available presets.
  • 10-band equalizer.
  • Word-by-word synchronized lyrics using supported LRC files.
  • Custom Now Playing scenes, including campfire, sakura, windmill and aurora.
  • Album-art-inspired colors and customizable seekbar styles.
  • Offline playback without internet access.
  • No advertisements or tracking.
