Beatzyy

Free Edition

Beatzyy Free is a free Android music player designed to provide music playback and audio customization features through an interface inspired by the classic Android Holo design language.

  • Android
  • Free
  • Upcoming
Coming soon to Google Play

This is the free edition of Beatzyy. The paid edition, Beatzyy — Slowed Reverb Player, is a separate application on Google Play.

Features

  • Slowed playback and nightcore.
  • Reverb effects and audio customization.
  • Music playback controls.
  • Classic Android-inspired interface.
  • Offline playback.
  • No advertisements, internet permission or application logging.
